Global Conventions

All cards are generated from BCP and New Recruit tournament data over a rolling 30-day window ending on the card's as-of date. Only events with parsed army lists are included. Draws are counted as half a win.

Minimum sample sizes — cells or rows with fewer than 10 games are rendered in grey to signal low confidence. Matchup panels require at least 5 games per matchup (10 for faction-level matchups) before showing a win rate; below that threshold the raw count n=3 is shown instead.

Wilson 95% CI Lower — "Best Into" panels rank matchups by the lower bound of a Wilson confidence interval rather than raw win rate, so a 90% win rate from 5 games doesn't beat a 70% win rate from 50 games.

Win Rate Color Bands

Every win rate value — badges, table cells, heatmap cells, and scatter points — uses the same three-band scheme:

≥ 55%▮ Favoured
46 – 54%▮ Even
≤ 45%▮ Unfavoured

Symbol Key

✓Best matchup sub-row in Detachment Breakdown — a favourable matchup for this detachment.
✗Worst matchup sub-row in Detachment Breakdown — an unfavourable matchup.
▲ / ▼Trend direction in the WIN RATE badge — win rate rose (▲) or fell (▼) vs. the previous equivalent window.
ΔImpact delta in Top Units — WR-with-unit minus WR-without-unit. A positive Δ means this unit is correlated with better results.
▸Section sub-header row inside a table (e.g. detachment name in Detachment Breakdown).
⚠Monoculture warning banner — shown when one detachment accounts for ≥ 75% of all faction lists. Means the faction's overall win rate is dominated by a single build.

Narrative Insights

The bullet list near the top of the card auto-generates up to five observations from the data. Each bullet fires only when the signal is strong enough to be meaningful:

  • Trend — win rate change vs. prior window (needs n ≥ 10 in both windows).
  • Top-table performance — flags "glass ceiling" (top-table WR ≥ 8 pp below overall) or "pressure performer" (≥ 8 pp above). Needs top_table_n ≥ 10.
  • Top-cut rate — flags "podium contender" (≥ 10%) or "undertuned" (< 5%).
  • First-turn preference — flags strong first- or second-turn bias when the gap is ≥ 8 pp and both sub-samples have ≥ 15 games.
  • High-impact unit — flags the top unit when its prevalence is ≥ 20% and its Δ is positive.

Best / Worst Into — Factions, Detachments, Archetypes

Horizontal bar charts showing the faction's best and worst historical matchups. Three variants appear on each card: vs. Factions, vs. Detachments, and vs. Archetypes (specific named builds).

Best Into — sorted by Wilson CI lower bound descending (most confidently favoured matchups first). Worst Into — sorted by raw win rate ascending (most lopsided losses first). The dashed line at 50% marks the even point.

Matchups with fewer than the minimum sample threshold are excluded entirely from these lists (not just greyed out).

Detachments

A table of every detachment played by this faction in the window, showing play share and win rate. Play Rate % is the percentage of faction lists using each detachment. Rows with fewer than 10 games are dimmed.

Top Players

Players with the most games on this faction in the window, sorted by game count then win rate. Requires at least 4 games to appear. Shows W-L record, WR%, best tournament finish, and their most-played detachment.

Top Units (sorted by WR impact Δ)

Units are grouped into three buckets by how commonly they appear:

▸ PILLAR ≥ 70% — found in most lists; likely a staple.
▸ CORE 30–70% — present in a large minority; a real choice.
▸ FLEX < 30% — situational or niche pick.

Within each bucket, units are sorted by |Δ| (absolute impact) then prevalence. The Δ column is the difference between the win rate of games where the unit was included vs. games where it was absent. A large positive Δ suggests the unit is correlated with better results; a large negative Δ suggests the opposite — though correlation is not causation (good players may skew toward or away from certain units).

Only units recognised by the BSData army builder registry are shown; free-text or OCR-parsed extras are filtered out.

Top Enhancements

The most-played character enhancements (wargear upgrades), sorted by play rate. Shows prevalence as a percentage of faction lists and overall win rate for games featuring each enhancement. Rows with fewer than 10 games are dimmed.

Win Rate by Round

The faction's win rate in each round of Swiss play (R1–R5 or R1–R6 depending on event format). The dashed line marks 50%. A declining curve across rounds can indicate the faction tends to face stronger opponents as the event progresses; a rising curve can indicate resilience or a strong late-game meta position.

Weekly WR Trend

Win rate bucketed into calendar weeks within the 30-day window, oldest left to newest right. Useful for spotting whether a faction is on the rise or decline within the current meta period. Weeks with very few games should be treated with caution — the dots are sized equally regardless of sample.

Scoring Shape · First Turn

Six statistics describing how this faction wins and loses, not just whether it does:

  • Win margin — average VP gap in games this faction won. Higher = more dominant wins.
  • Loss margin — average VP gap in games this faction lost. Smaller = more competitive losses.
  • Tabling rate — % of wins where the opponent scored ≤ 3 VP (effectively tabled). Green when ≥ 15%.
  • Close games (≤ 5 VP) — % of all games decided by 5 VP or fewer. A high rate means the faction often plays razor-thin matches.
  • Blowouts (≥ 15 VP) — % of all games decided by 15 VP or more in either direction.
  • Going first / Going second — win rate when the faction takes the first or second turn. Only shown when both sub-samples have ≥ 5 games. A large gap signals strong first- or second-turn dependence.

Detachment Breakdown — Win Rate & Matchups

A drilldown for the top 3 most-played detachments. Each detachment row shows its overall win rate and list count. Below it, sub-rows list the two best (✓) and two worst (✗) matchups for that specific detachment, giving a sense of how different builds within the faction play differently into the meta. Requires at least 5 games per matchup.

Detachment Heatmap — WR % vs. Opponent Faction

A grid showing the win rate of up to 5 detachments (rows) against up to 8 of the most-played opponent factions (columns). Each cell is coloured:

65%   WR ≥ 55% — favoured
50%   WR 46–54% — even
38%   WR ≤ 45% — unfavoured

Cells with fewer than 5 games show n=X instead of a win rate; cells with zero games show —. The heatmap lets you compare which detachment of a given faction fares best into a specific opponent.
