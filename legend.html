About BCPTrawler

BCPTrawler collects and analyses competitive Warhammer 40,000 tournament data from Best Coast Pairings (BCP) and New Recruit events. It provides win rates, play rates, detachment breakdowns, top player rankings, matchup data, and weekly meta trends — all from real tournament results.

Data is refreshed periodically. The window label in the header shows the date range the current view covers.

Event Types

All Events

Combines solo (individual) and team tournament results. Largest sample size.

Singles

Individual (1v1) tournament results only. Reflects solo competitive meta.

Teams

Team event results only. Pairing dynamics and team composition affect outcomes, so meta may differ from solo.

Metric Definitions

Play Rate %

The percentage of all army lists in the window that play this faction. A faction with a 10% play rate appeared in 10 out of every 100 lists.

Win Rate %

Win rate across all games in the window. A draw counts as 0.5 wins. Calculated from the perspective of the faction's player, not their opponent.

50% is even. Above 55% is strong; below 45% is weak. Colour-coded: green ≥ 55%, yellow 45–55%, red ≤ 45%.

Trend

Win-rate change compared to the previous window of the same length. For example, with a 30-day window, Trend compares the most recent 30 days to the 30 days before that.

▲ means improving, ▼ means declining. A small triangle (±0.5%) is considered flat (shown as —).

Top Detachment

The detachment used by the most lists for this faction in the window. Not necessarily the strongest — just the most popular.

Lists

Total number of army lists recorded for this faction or detachment in the window. Each player at each event contributes one list.

Games

Total number of individual game results recorded. Each round of an event produces one game per player.

Faction Detail Page

Detachment Breakdown

Lists and win rate split by detachment (formation). Play % here is relative to this faction's own lists, not all lists.

Win Rate into Opponent

This faction's win rate against each specific opponent faction. Only matchups with at least 5 games are shown. Sorted best to worst.

Top Players

Players ranked by win rate with this faction in the window. Minimum 3 games required to appear. A "View List" link (where available) opens the original BCP or New Recruit list.

Lists per Week / Win Rate per Week

Weekly breakdown of list count and win rate. Shows participation trends and whether performance has shifted recently. Each point is one calendar week.

Data Sources & Caveats

  • Sources: Best Coast Pairings (BCP) and New Recruit tournament platforms. Data covers GT-level and above events in the window.
  • Army list parsing: Lists are parsed from BCP and New Recruit export formats. Faction and detachment are extracted from the list text, not the BCP roster (which can be inaccurate for some factions).
  • Sample sizes: Win rates for factions or detachments with small samples (< 20 games) should be treated with caution — they appear in the data but can swing significantly. Matchups require at least 5 games; player rankings require at least 3.
  • Team events: Team pairing dynamics differ from solo play. Win rates in Teams mode reflect performance within the team pairing context.
  • Refresh cadence: Data is rebuilt periodically after new events are processed. The build date in the footer shows when the current data was generated.
