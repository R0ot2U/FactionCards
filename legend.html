About Informed Crusader

Informed Crusader collects and analyses competitive Warhammer 40,000 tournament data from Best Coast Pairings (BCP) and New Recruit events. It provides win rates, play rates, detachment breakdowns, top player rankings, matchup data, player ELO ratings, and weekly meta trends — all from real tournament results.

Data is refreshed periodically after tournament weekends. Time windows are anchored to the most recent event date, not today's calendar date, ensuring you always see complete tournament data rather than empty windows.

Event Types

All Events

Combines solo (individual) and team tournament results. Largest sample size.

Singles

Individual (1v1) tournament results only. Reflects solo competitive meta.

Teams

Team event results only. Pairing dynamics and team composition affect outcomes, so meta may differ from solo.

Metric Definitions

Play Rate %

The percentage of all players in the window using this faction. A faction with a 10% play rate means 10 out of every 100 players used it.

Win Rate %

Win rate across all games in the window. A draw counts as 0.5 wins. Calculated from the perspective of the faction's player, not their opponent.

50% is even. Above 55% is strong; below 45% is weak. Colour-coded: green ≥ 55%, yellow 45–55%, red ≤ 45%.

Meta Health

Statistical assessment of faction strength based on Wilson 95% confidence interval (CI) lower bound. Uses a pessimistic estimate to avoid over-rating factions with small samples.

5 tiers: Strongly Over (CI > 55%), Slightly Over (CI > 50%), Neutral (50%), Slightly Under (CI < 50%), Strongly Under (CI < 45%). A 51.5% win rate can show "underperform" if the CI lower bound is below 50%.

Trend (Factions)

Win-rate change compared to the previous window of the same length. For example, with a 30-day window, Trend compares the most recent 30 days to the 30 days before that. Shows whether the faction's performance is improving or declining.

▲ means improving, ▼ means declining. Shown as percentage points (e.g., +3.2%). A small change (±0.5%) is considered flat (shown as —).

Trend (Players)

ELO rating change within the current window. Calculated as rating after the last game minus rating before the first game in the window. Shows whether a player's skill rating rose or fell.

Measured in rating points (e.g., +258.8), not percentage. ▲ means rating increased, ▼ means decreased. A small change (±0.5 points) is considered flat (shown as —).

X-0 Rate

Percentage of players who went undefeated with this faction (0 losses, 0 draws, at least 1 win). Measures ceiling potential — how often does the faction take players to perfect records?

X-1 Rate

Percentage of players who dropped exactly one game — either 1 loss and 0 draws, or 0 losses and 1 draw. Combined with X-0, this shows how many players finished with strong records (top tables).

Tournament Wins

Number of 1st place finishes (lists with placing = 1). Direct measure of championship performance. Shown both faction-wide and per detachment.

Top Detachment

The detachment used by the most players for this faction in the window. Not necessarily the strongest — just the most popular.

Players

Total number of players recorded for this faction or detachment in the window. Each player at each event contributes one entry.

Games

Total number of individual game results recorded. Each round of an event produces one game per player.

Player Rankings

ELO Rating

Skill rating based on game results and opponent strength. All players start at 1500. Higher rating means stronger competitive performance.

Beating a higher-rated opponent increases your rating more than beating a lower-rated opponent. Losing to a lower-rated opponent decreases your rating more than losing to a higher-rated opponent.

Peak Rating

Highest ELO rating this player has ever achieved across all events in the database. Shows their best historical performance.

K-Factor

How volatile the player's rating is. Determines how much their rating changes after each game.

40 = new player (fewer than 30 games), 32 = standard (30+ games), 24 = experienced (100+ games). Higher K means bigger rating swings per game.

Consistency

Score from 0 to 1 measuring how stable a player's performance is across events. Higher = more consistent results.

Calculated from variance in win rates across multiple events. A player who consistently goes 3-2 has higher consistency than a player who alternates between 5-0 and 1-4.

Upset Rate

Win rate specifically against higher-rated opponents. Measures a player's ability to punch above their weight class.

Only counts games where the opponent's rating was higher than the player's rating at the time of the match. A 40% upset rate means winning 2 out of every 5 games against stronger opponents.

Trend

Rating change over the current window. See "Trend (Players)" in Metric Definitions above for details.

Tournament Map

Statistical Methodology

Wilson Confidence Interval

Meta Health uses the Wilson 95% confidence interval (CI) instead of raw win rate percentages. The CI gives a range estimate for the "true" win rate, accounting for sample size.

A faction with 51.5% win rate but only 20 games might have a CI of 45%–58%, meaning the true win rate could plausibly be anywhere in that range. We use the lower bound (45%) for a pessimistic, conservative assessment.

Why CI Lower Bound?

Using the CI lower bound prevents over-rating factions with lucky small samples. A faction with 60% win rate over 10 games has a wide CI (e.g., 47%–73%), so the lower bound keeps the health rating cautious until more data confirms the performance.

Incomplete Data Filtering

Players with no game records (wins + losses + draws = 0) are excluded from X-0/X-1/Tournament Wins calculations. These represent unparsed or incomplete data, often from events where list scraping failed.

Data Sources & Caveats

  • Sources: Best Coast Pairings (BCP) and New Recruit tournament platforms. Data covers GT-level and above events in the window.
  • Army list parsing: Player lists are parsed from BCP and New Recruit export formats. Faction and detachment are extracted from the list text, not the BCP roster (which can be inaccurate for some factions).
  • Sample sizes: Win rates for factions or detachments with small samples (< 20 games) should be treated with caution — they appear in the data but can swing significantly. Matchups require at least 5 games; player rankings require at least 3.
  • Team events: Team pairing dynamics differ from solo play. Win rates in Teams mode reflect performance within the team pairing context.
  • Refresh cadence: Data is rebuilt periodically after new events are processed. The build date in the footer shows when the current data was generated.
  • Time window anchoring: Windows (7d, 14d, 30d, 60d) are anchored to the most recent event date in the dataset, not today's calendar date. This ensures windows always show complete tournament data rather than empty periods. For example, if the last data refresh was Tuesday May 20 after a weekend of events, the "7 days" window shows May 13–20 (the week of tournament data), not May 17–24 (which would be empty).
